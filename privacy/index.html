3. Website Hosting

3.1.

The website is intended to be hosted on Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (“Cloudflare”).

3.2.

When you visit the website, your browser automatically sends technical information that is needed to deliver the pages. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of the request
  • Requested page or file
  • Browser and operating system information
  • Referring page (referrer)
  • Error and security-related information
3.3.

Requests are handled by Cloudflare's global network, usually by a data center close to the visitor. This can be outside the EU. Cloudflare also uses this information to protect the website against attacks and misuse.

3.4.

This information is used to deliver the website, keep it secure and investigate technical problems. It is not used to identify visitors or build profiles.

3.5.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is providing a working and secure website.

3.6.

Cloudflare processes this data on behalf of Abyss Development as a processor. The data processing agreement required by Article 28 GDPR is Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum, (version 6.4, effective April 3, 2026). It forms part of Cloudflare's Self-Serve Subscription Agreement, under which Abyss Development uses Cloudflare. Abyss Development remains responsible for this processing as the controller. More information: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Pending: Cloudflare's logging and log retention for this website (see Section 5.7).

9. Transfers Outside the EU

9.1.

Some providers process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), in particular in the USA. Such transfers must comply with Chapter V GDPR, for example through an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Article 45 GDPR, including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified US companies) or Standard Contractual Clauses (Article 46 GDPR).

9.2.

Cloudflare: Cloudflare, Inc. is based in the USA, and Cloudflare and its sub-processors may process data outside the EEA. Cloudflare states in its GDPR documentation that it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so transfers to Cloudflare in the USA can be based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Article 45 GDPR). Where a transfer is not covered by it, Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with supplementary safeguards (Article 46 GDPR). For self-serve customers, these transfer mechanisms apply through the agreement without any separate step. Data location settings for the planned client area are not yet decided (see Section 5.7).

9.3.

Bot-Hosting.net: Bot-Hosting.net states that it runs deployments on servers in the EU and the US, uses US subcontractors, and relies on Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers outside the EU.

9.4.

Microsoft, Discord and PayPal act as independent controllers and are responsible for their own transfers outside the EEA. Each describes the mechanisms it uses in its privacy statement. Discord, for example, states that it stores data in the USA and relies on Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

10. Data Retention

10.1.

Personal data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for. It is kept longer only where a statutory retention obligation applies, and then only for the data covered by that obligation.

10.2.

Inquiries and communication, including emails, are kept only as long as needed to handle the inquiry, maintain the business relationship or resolve related matters, such as warranty or legal claims. Where parts of it are subject to a statutory retention obligation (see Section 10.3), those parts are kept for the legal period. Data that is no longer needed and is not subject to a retention obligation is deleted or anonymized.

10.3.

Tax-relevant records are kept for the periods set by § 147 AO, counted from the end of the calendar year in which they were created:

  • 10 years for books and records, such as the annual accounts
  • 8 years for booking documents (Buchungsbelege), such as invoices
  • 6 years for received and sent business letters and other records relevant for taxation

These periods apply only to documents in these categories. Other personal data is not kept for these periods.

10.4.

Discord messages remain on Discord until they are deleted. Discord's own retention rules apply.

10.5.

When the contract with Cloudflare ends, Cloudflare deletes or returns the personal data it processes on behalf of Abyss Development, unless it is legally required to keep it. Pending: retention of Cloudflare logs and client area data (see Section 5.7) and of Bot-Hosting.net logs (see Section 7.3).

13. Your Rights

13.1.

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access to your personal data (Article 15)
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data (Article 16)
  • Erasure, where the legal requirements are met (Article 17)
  • Restriction of processing (Article 18)
  • Data portability (Article 20)
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time, with effect for the future (Article 7(3)). No processing described in this policy is currently based on consent.
13.2.

Right to object (Article 21 GDPR)

Where data is processed on the basis of legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), you have the right to object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. Abyss Development will then stop processing that data, unless there are compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

13.3.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), for example the authority where you live. The authority responsible for Abyss Development is:
