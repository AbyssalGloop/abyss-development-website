A little about me

I'm an independent developer with a focus on Discord bots, game integrations and automation.

What started with working on a community's own bot gradually grew into something more. Over time, I've gained hands-on experience maintaining existing systems, adding new features and building tools that make running gaming communities easier.

I enjoy figuring out how things work and finding practical ways to bring ideas to life. I'm always looking to learn something new, take on interesting challenges and improve along the way.

Now I'd like to put that experience to use and help other communities build what they have in mind.

  • Abyss Development is just me, so you'll be talking directly to the person writing the code.

  • Most of what I build is written in JavaScript, mainly Discord bots and tools that connect games with Discord.

  • I mostly work on the parts that run behind the scenes, like commands, data handling and the logic that keeps everything working.

  • I'm self-taught and keep learning as I go. Every project teaches me something new.

Featured project

Ignited Realms Bot

A custom Discord bot developed and maintained for a gaming community, combining Discord functionality with game integrations, automation and community management tools.

  • JavaScript
  • Node.js
  • MySQL
  • Player Profile
  • Growth Station
  • Targeting Detection
  • Moderation Tools
  • Map Voting
  • Banking Station (video)
    • Connects Discord with the community's Path of Titans servers through RCON, the game's server API and webhooks.
    • Lets players link their in-game account to Discord through a verification panel.
    • In-game chat commands for map voting, waystone teleports, the shop, duels and reports.
    • Map votes that switch the map and restart the server automatically.
    • Runs in-game events like Dark Time and Migration.
    • Checks that the servers come back online after a restart and lets staff know if something goes wrong.
    • Moderates global in-game chat with banned and muted word lists.
    • Detects third-partying — when outside players join an ongoing fight — while distinguishing them from actual groupmates.
    • Flags possible ban evasion and players who keep targeting the same person, so staff can take a look.
    • Keeps separate warning, mute and ban records for Discord and in-game.
    • Staff tools for notes, investigations, admin logs and clock-in tracking.
    • Staff applications handled directly in Discord.
    • An in-game currency, Magma Shards, earned through activity, voting, weekly quests and events.
    • A shop for items and weather changes, usable in-game or from a Discord panel.
    • Minigames like dice, slots and blackjack, played with the in-game currency.
    • Weekly quests with three tiers, so casual players still earn something.
    • Player profiles, leaderboards and survival stats.
    • Announcements for new YouTube videos from the community and its creators.
    • The bot is still in active development, with new features and fixes going in regularly.
    • Features and improvements are developed around the community's needs.
    • Recent additions include a separate chat strike system and a buff for players who play solo.

These ranges are starting points, not fixed prices. The game, your existing setup and the overall complexity can all affect the final quote. Smaller projects can be paid upfront, while larger projects can be arranged through milestone payments.
