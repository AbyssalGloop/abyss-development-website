A little about me

I'm an independent developer with a focus on Discord bots, game integrations and automation.

What started with working on a community's own bot gradually grew into something more. Over time, I've gained hands-on experience maintaining existing systems, adding new features and building tools that make running gaming communities easier.

I enjoy figuring out how things work and finding practical ways to bring ideas to life. I'm always looking to learn something new, take on interesting challenges and improve along the way.

Now I'd like to put that experience to use and help other communities build what they have in mind.

  • Abyss Development is just me, so you'll be talking directly to the person writing the code.

  • Most of what I build is written in JavaScript, mainly Discord bots and tools that connect games with Discord.

  • I mostly work on the parts that run behind the scenes, like commands, data handling and the logic that keeps everything working.

  • I'm self-taught and keep learning as I go. Every project teaches me something new.

  • JavaScript

    • Weekly quests with three tiers, so casual players still earn something.
    • Player profiles, leaderboards and survival stats.

Pricing

Every project is different. Whether you need a small Discord feature, a game integration or a more extensive system, pricing depends on the work involved and the requirements of your project.

The examples below are intended as a general guide, not fixed offers. Final pricing is discussed individually before work begins.

1

Discord-only Development

Projects that run entirely within Discord, without a connection to a game server.

  • Small adjustments / commands (slash commands, buttons, role changes) €30–70
  • Ticket and support systems €40–100
  • Moderation and warning systems €50–150
  • More involved Discord systems (combined moderation, ticket and staff tools) €150–400
2

Game-integrated Development

Projects that communicate with a game server through available interfaces such as RCON, APIs or webhooks.

  • Player verification and account linking €80–250
  • Player statistics and tracking €100–250
  • In-game commands (teleport, shops, etc.) €100–300+
  • Automated server management (map voting, server monitoring) €150–400+

These are examples rather than fixed prices. Actual costs depend on the game's available interfaces, existing infrastructure, complexity and required functionality.

+

Optional game integration

Game integration can also be added to projects that would otherwise be Discord-only. For example, a Discord moderation bot could optionally include:

  • In-game account linking
  • Player lookups connecting Discord users to their in-game identities
  • Synchronisation of warnings, bans and mutes between Discord and the game

Game integration is not mandatory for every project. Its requirements and additional development costs depend on the game and available interfaces.

Larger projects & complete systems

More extensive bots, interconnected systems and complete custom projects are quoted individually based on their scope, complexity and requirements. The examples above cover individual features and smaller systems, not complete bots like the one in the project showcase.
